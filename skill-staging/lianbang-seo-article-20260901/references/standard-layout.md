# 連邦文章標準版型

The authoritative reusable source is `../assets/standard-article-template.html`. Use its CSS and section rhythm as the default, then replace all condition-specific text, anchors, alt text, and image filenames.

## Layout

- Desktop outer shell: 1180px maximum.
- Primary aligned content: 1040px maximum.
- Long-form reading sections: 980px maximum.
- Mobile gutters: 16px per side.
- Keep the title, copy, figures, lists, navigation, and CTA on consistent left edges.
- Stack the title and hero illustration vertically. Do not use offset, floating, overlapping, or sticky side content.
- Use simple two-column lists only inside the aligned reading width; collapse them to one column on mobile.

## Visual language

- Warm ivory page, restrained teal headings, fine neutral separators.
- Traditional Chinese serif headings and highly readable sans-serif body copy.
- Images use 12px corners and a light shadow. Do not vary corner shapes between sections.
- Avoid card grids, oversized color blocks, ornamental circles, arbitrary English kickers, emojis in headings, and dense metadata.

## Content and images

- Default to four topic-specific inline images when no image quantity is supplied: opening overview, mechanism or symptoms, treatment approach, and daily care or practical context.
- Use 1200×800 WebP plus JPEG for inline images, ideally below 200KB each.
- Place every image beside or immediately after the section it explains.
- Keep visible image captions absent. Use descriptive Traditional Chinese `alt` text instead.
- Do not copy patient stories, outcomes, evidence claims, or medical facts from the template topic into another article.

## Copy preferences

- Titles are plain titles, without AI-style subtitles or explanatory labels.
- Do not add source-reference sections, generic disclaimer paragraphs, red-flag panels, or formulaic “record symptoms and discuss with a doctor” copy unless explicitly requested.
- Keep the prose professional and direct. State what the condition is, common presentations, the clinic's TCM assessment, treatment methods, daily care, and FAQ when supported by source material.

## Publication

- Preview locally and receive explicit user approval before updating WordPress.
- Preserve the existing permalink and confirmed clinic contact details.
- After publication, verify one H1, image delivery, CTA links, desktop alignment, mobile layout, and frontend cache propagation.
